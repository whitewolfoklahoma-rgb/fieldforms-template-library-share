How this library works

FieldForms Template Library Vol. 1 is the companion to FieldForms Toolkit. The Toolkit covers change orders, punch lists, daily logs, and job safety assessments. This library covers the other paperwork — the documents that frame a job before it starts and close it out when it ends:

  • Bid / Proposal Cover Sheet — scope, exclusions, allowances, payment schedule, and an acceptance signature, so a "yes" is on paper.
  • Subcontractor Agreement (short form) — scope, pay terms, and insurance/license representations with signatures.
  • Warranty Letter — your workmanship warranty in writing: what's covered, what isn't, and how to make a claim.
  • Final Walkthrough & Acceptance Certificate — the customer's signed statement that the job is complete and accepted. (Different from a punch list: this is the finish line, not the to-do list.)
  • Lien Waiver — conditional or unconditional, progress or final payment variants.
  • Incident / Near-Miss Report — site safety documentation for injuries, near misses, and damage.
  • Delivery Receipt Log — who received what material, when, and in what condition.
  • Testimonial & Photo Release — written permission to use a happy customer's words and job photos in your marketing.

Workflow

Fill in the form, tap Preview sheet to see exactly what prints, then Print / save PDF. Signatures are typed names printed in script style on the signature line — for maximum formality, have the signer also sign the printed sheet by hand. Use Duplicate in the library to reuse a good document's wording on the next job.

About the legal templates — read this

These templates are general-purpose forms, not legal advice. They do not create an attorney–client relationship, and no template can fit every state, trade, or situation. Lien-waiver rules in particular vary by state — several states require their own statutory forms. Have a licensed attorney in your state review the subcontractor agreement and lien waiver before you rely on them, and follow your state's rules where they exist.
